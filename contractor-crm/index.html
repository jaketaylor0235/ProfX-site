Contractor CRM FAQ

Common questions about contractor CRM software.

What is a contractor CRM?

A contractor CRM is software that helps home service businesses manage leads, customers, sales opportunities, quotes, follow-up, communication, and revenue. ProfX also connects that CRM directly to scheduling, dispatch, field operations, invoices, and payments.

Can ProfX help stop quotes from going cold?

Yes. ProfX supports automated text follow-up cadences and opportunity monitoring designed to keep open quotes visible and moving until the customer responds or the opportunity is resolved.

Is ProfX only a sales CRM?

No. The CRM connects to scheduling, dispatch, employee and crew assignment, field notes, job photos, customer communication, invoicing, payments, and operational reporting.

What types of contractors can use ProfX?

ProfX is designed for home service and contracting companies including roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, solar, landscaping, and general contracting businesses.

Does ProfX include texting?

Yes. ProfX includes two-way customer texting and automated SMS follow-up workflows so customer communication stays connected to the CRM.
