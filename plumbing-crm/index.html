Plumbing CRM FAQ

Common questions from plumbing contractors.

What is plumbing CRM software?

Plumbing CRM software helps plumbing businesses manage leads, customers, estimates, sales follow-up, scheduling, dispatch, communication, jobs, technicians, invoices, and revenue.

Can ProfX help with larger plumbing estimates?

Yes. ProfX can keep open project estimates in structured follow-up and surface stale opportunities so sales activity does not disappear behind day-to-day service work.

Does ProfX support technician workflows?

Yes. Field staff can access assigned jobs, customer information, directions, notes, photos, job status, and completion workflows.

Yes. Two-way SMS and automated reminders keep customer communication connected to the CRM and job record.
