HVAC CRM FAQ

Common questions from HVAC contractors.

What is HVAC CRM software?

HVAC CRM software helps heating and cooling companies manage leads, customers, estimates, follow-up, scheduling, dispatch, technicians, communication, jobs, invoices, and revenue.

Can ProfX help with HVAC replacement sales?

Yes. ProfX tracks open opportunities, supports automated SMS follow-up, shows stale estimates, and gives sales teams visibility into which replacement quotes need action.

Does ProfX include dispatch?

Yes. Jobs can be scheduled and assigned to employees or crews, with customer information, notes, directions, photos, and completion workflows available to field staff.

Can customers receive reminders by text?

Yes. ProfX supports customer texting and automated reminders so appointment communication can stay consistent.
