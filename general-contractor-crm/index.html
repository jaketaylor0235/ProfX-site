General Contractor CRM FAQ

Common questions from general contractors.

What is general contractor CRM software?

It is software that helps contractors manage leads, customers, estimates, sales follow-up, schedules, jobs, crews, communication, field documentation, invoices, and revenue.

Can ProfX help prevent project estimates from going cold?

Yes. ProfX supports automated text cadences and opportunity monitoring so sales teams can keep open estimates moving and see which deals need attention.

Does ProfX support job photos and field notes?

Yes. Field teams can add notes and photos to jobs, including before, progress, and after documentation, while office staff keep visibility into project status.

Can ProfX handle scheduling and crews?

Yes. Approved work can move into scheduling with employee and crew assignments tied to the same job record.
