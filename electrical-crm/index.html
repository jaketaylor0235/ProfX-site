Electrical CRM FAQ

It is software that helps electrical companies manage leads, customers, estimates, follow-up, scheduling, field assignments, communication, invoices, and revenue.

Yes. ProfX tracks open opportunities, quote value, follow-up activity, pipeline stage, and lost-deal reasons so larger projects remain visible.

Yes. Employees can access assigned jobs, customer information, directions, notes, photos, status, and completion workflows.

Yes. Two-way customer texting and automated follow-up keep communication connected to the CRM and job record.
