Partnership Principle

Useful first. Branded second.

ProfX should not force a contractor into a partner product simply because a partnership exists. Any integration, resource or co-marketing relationship should be transparent, relevant to the contractor's workflow and based on real capabilities. This page describes partnership opportunities — it does not imply that ProfX currently has a commercial relationship with any company not specifically announced by ProfX.
