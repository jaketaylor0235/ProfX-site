From Sale to Completed Job

Your CRM shouldn't stop working when the deal closes.

ProfX connects the sales side of your business directly to scheduling, dispatch, field operations, customer communication, invoicing, and completion — eliminating the handoff between disconnected systems.

Lead → Opportunity

Capture customers and move opportunities through a structured sales pipeline.

Opportunity → Quote

Build estimates, send customer approval links, and track the sales process.

Quote → Follow-Up

Automated text cadences help keep open estimates from quietly going cold.

Approval → Scheduling

Move sold work into scheduling and production without losing the handoff.

Scheduling → Dispatch

Coordinate employees, crews, calendars, customer requests, and job timing.

Dispatch → Field

Give technicians the job information, notes, photos, directions, and tools they need.

Field → Completion

Track progress, job photos, field notes, statuses, and completion workflows.

Completion → Customer Care

Keep final billing, payment, reviews, warranty, referrals, service requests, and future follow-up connected.
